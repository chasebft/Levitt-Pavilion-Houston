01 Logo

The Levitt Pavilion Houston mark combines concentric arcs, evoking sound waves, a stage canopy, and community radiating outward, with a central star representing the performer at the heart of every gathering.

Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the star element. No other graphic elements, text, or visual clutter should intrude into this zone.

Minimum Size

Primary logo: no smaller than 120px wide for digital, 1.5" for print. Alternate mark: no smaller than 32px for digital, 0.5" for print.

Do

  • Use on solid, uncluttered backgrounds
  • Maintain original proportions
  • Use the alternate mark for small spaces (social avatars, favicons)
  • Pair with brand-approved colors only

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, or skew the logo
  • Place over busy photography without a backing shape
  • Alter the arc or star proportions
  • Apply drop shadows, gradients, or effects
  • Recreate or approximate the logo in other typefaces

Accessibility Usage

Pavilion Green is an accent color, not a text color

At 3.1:1 on white, the green passes AA only at large/bold sizes (24px+ or 19px bold). Never use it for body copy on light backgrounds. For readable text, always use Stage Navy on white or white on navy. The green is for: logos, icons, buttons, borders, highlights, and display headings on dark backgrounds.

Do

  • Use Stage Navy for all body text on light backgrounds
  • Use white text on navy or dark backgrounds
  • Use Pavilion Green for accents: buttons, icons, borders, highlights
  • Green text is OK on navy backgrounds and at display sizes on white

Don't

  • Use Pavilion Green for body text on white (fails AA)
  • Use green text below 24px on light backgrounds
  • Place green on Soft Mint (insufficient contrast)
  • Introduce off-brand colors without approval

04 Voice & Tone

We sound like an enthusiastic neighbor who happens to be throwing the best block party in Houston. Warm, genuine, and unapologetically energetic.

Welcoming

Everyone belongs here. Language should feel like an open invitation, never exclusive or gatekeeping. No jargon, no pretense.

Energetic

We're excited about what we do, and it shows. Copy should have rhythm and momentum without crossing into hype.

Community-First

The audience is the protagonist, not the organization. Center neighbors, families, artists, and the shared experience.

Grounded

Houston pride without cliché. Specific to place. Name neighborhoods, reference local culture, stay rooted.

Say

  • "Bring your lawn chair and your crew"
  • "Free shows all season long"
  • "Music that brings us together"
  • "Your neighborhood, your stage"

Don't Say

  • "An exclusive cultural experience"
  • "Leveraging music to drive engagement"
  • "A premier destination for..."
  • Overly formal or corporate phrasing

05 Application Guidelines

How the brand comes to life across touchpoints, from social media to event signage.

Social Media

Use the alternate mark as profile avatar. Apply Pavilion Green as the dominant color in cover images. Keep text overlays in Roboto bold. Event flyers can use the full accent palette.

Event Signage

Primary logo at main stage and entrance. Minimum 18" width for outdoor banners. High contrast: green on white or white on navy for visibility at distance.

Print Materials

Flyers, postcards, and programs should lead with imagery and use the logo at a consistent size in the header or footer. Body text in Roboto at 10pt minimum.

Merchandise

The alternate mark works well for embroidery and small-scale print (hats, pins). For t-shirts, either the full logo or a simplified one-color version in white or Pavilion Green.
