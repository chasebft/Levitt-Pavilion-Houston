Our Logo Marks

Two marks, one mission. A quick introduction to their usage and meaning.

01

The Word Mark

Our primary identity. Concentric arcs and a central star paired with the wordmark. The default for all brand touchpoints.

Word Mark 02

Word Mark on Navy

White logotype with green accents for dark backgrounds. Full legibility maintained at every scale.

Word Mark 03

The Graphic Mark

A compact mark for tight spaces. Social avatars, favicons, merchandise, and embroidery.

Graphic Mark 04

Graphic Mark on Navy

The icon on dark surfaces. Holds clarity at small sizes and single-color applications.

Graphic Mark 05

Logo Elements

The four core elements that make up the Levitt Pavilion Houston mark.

Sound Arcs

Sound waves radiating outward from a stage. Community expanding from a shared center.

Lone Star

The performer at the heart of every gathering. A focal point where energy gathers.

Derrick Tower

A nod to Houston's industrial heritage. Repurposed as a beacon for community and rooted in place.

Bayou Wave

Houston's waterways connecting neighborhoods to the stage and to each other.
